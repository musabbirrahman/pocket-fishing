# 🎣 Pocket Fishing

A cozy, relaxing browser fishing game. No install, no dependencies: just open `index.html`.

## Features
- 4 ponds: Meadow Pond, Misty Lake, Moonlit Sea, Jungle Lagoon
- Day/night cycle and changing weather (clear, cloudy, rain, fog)
- Fish swim in the water, notice your bait, nibble, then bite
- Bait shop with coins, unlockable baits, and a free worm dig
- Collection book, fishing journal, and ambient nature sounds

## How to play
Tap where you want to cast. When the bobber dips and "!" appears, tap again to reel in.
Turn on sound with the 🔊 button.

Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage).

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in any modern browser.
